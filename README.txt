HOW TO UPDATE YOUR PORTFOLIO
============================

Folder layout (keep it exactly like this):

  index.html
  assets/
    cv/
      Nirvay_Kumar_Bikram_CV.pdf     <- your CV
    images/
      profile.jpg                    <- your profile photo

Update your CV
  Replace assets/cv/Nirvay_Kumar_Bikram_CV.pdf with your new PDF.
  Keep the same file name. Both "Download CV" buttons pick it up.

Update your photo
  Replace assets/images/profile.jpg with your new photo.
  Keep the same file name. A square photo with your face in the upper half works best.

Use different file names?
  Open index.html, search for "assets/" and change the paths.
  The photo appears twice (hero and top navigation bar) and the CV link twice
  (hero and top navigation bar).

If you still see the old photo or CV after replacing a file, refresh with
Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac).

To publish: upload the whole folder (index.html and the assets folder together).
